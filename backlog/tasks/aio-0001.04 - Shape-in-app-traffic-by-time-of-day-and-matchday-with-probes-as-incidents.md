## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Load generator traffic is a flat Poisson stream all day and 5% of first questions carry a prompt-injection "Tool note" at a constant rate, so the dashboards show a flat line and guard warnings look mechanical. A real sports site peaks around kick-off and in the evening, and abuse arrives in bursts. The daily budget guard must keep bounding spend.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
