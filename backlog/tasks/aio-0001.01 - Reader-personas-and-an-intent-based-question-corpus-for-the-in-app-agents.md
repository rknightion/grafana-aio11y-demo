## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The load generator (`apps/agents/src/loadgen.mjs`) builds every question from seven formal templates such as "Summarize team news for X vs Y.", uses six faceless `reader-a`..`reader-f` ids, and sends one fixed follow-up. The `site-browser` CronJob (`apps/site/src/browser.mjs`) has the same shape. Real readers of a sports site ask short, messy, opinionated questions, come back with follow-ups that depend on the answer they got, and rate answers unevenly. The corpus must stay committed and reviewable so the demo is predictable on stage.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [x] #1 Questions come from a committed corpus of reader personas (for example casual fan, price-chasing punter, fantasy player, journalist) and intents, with several natural phrasings per intent including short, lowercase and misspelt forms
- [x] #2 Follow-ups are chosen per intent and read as a reply to the previous answer, not a fixed sentence
- [x] #3 Reader ids are stable per persona and ratings vary by persona and by answer outcome
- [x] #4 The site-browser CronJob draws from the same corpus
- [x] #5 The prompt-injection probe questions still reach the injection guard, and the existing loadgen tests are updated rather than deleted
