# Betting glossary (editorial reference)

- **each-way** - a bet split between winning and placing.
- **accumulator** - a single bet linking multiple selections; all must win.
- **draw no bet** - a bet refunded if the match is drawn.
- **handicap** - a virtual head start/deficit applied before settling the bet.
- **over/under** - a bet on whether a total (goals, points) is above or below a line.
